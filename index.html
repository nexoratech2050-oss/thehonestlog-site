§ 03 · the pact

Here's what won't happen.

No. There is no streak to break.

Miss a day and nothing resets. The one mechanic that makes people delete the app is not in this one.

No. Your first entry is not a search box.

Say it or snap it. No hunting a database for "chicken breast, raw, boneless, one." Ten seconds per meal, most of the time.

No. Every number shows its source.

Every estimate is checkable — brand label, USDA entry, your correction. You can trust the ones you audited, ignore the ones you didn't.

No. Your best feature will not move behind a paywall.

What you sign up for is what you get. If we ever charge for it, we tell you first and never quietly move an existing feature.
